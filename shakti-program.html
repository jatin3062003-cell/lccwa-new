LCCWA Initiatives

Shakti: Empowering Women

A specialized wing focused on bringing livelihood, financial literacy, and absolute community independence to women.

Self-Sufficiency & Independence

Shakti is a dedicated initiative for women empowerment championed by Ms. Sarita Rustogi. The core purpose of this movement is to help women from underprivileged backgrounds break systemic patterns, uncover inner creativity, and evolve into financially independent pillars of their households.

Initiative Lead

Ms. Sarita Rustogi

Key Pillars of Shakti

How we structure livelihood enablement

Empowerment & Education

Provides underprivileged neighborhood women with essential foundational literacy, numeric understanding, and modern skill training models.

Financial Independence

Enables true household self-sufficiency through creative, revenue-generating handicrafts like crafting custom Diyas, modern bags, bookmarks, and keychains.

Direct Market Access

Offers concrete opportunities to present and retail finished handmade inventory by linking artisan efforts directly with verified consumers.

Deep Community Bond

Many active women within Shakti are mothers of enrolled LCCWA classroom youth, which reinforces a deeply integrated, resilient neighborhood safety network.

Dual-Impact Nutrition Initiative

The talented women of Shakti take direct operational charge of managing standard kitchens to prepare freshly cooked, balanced evening nutritional meals for over 300+ LCCWA students daily. This closes an impact loop: securing student health metrics while directly augmenting independent monthly household incomes for the mothers.
