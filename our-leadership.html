In Loving Memory

People We Miss

Late Prof. Amitabh Gupta

Retired Professor, IIT Bombay & Founding Member

Foundation Pillar

Prof. Gupta can be directly credited for molding LCCWA into the structured, impactful institution that it represents today. Powered by a immense passion for childhood learning, he was deeply moved upon observing that brilliant children from low-income backgrounds continuously dropped out of schools—often pushed into child labor or early marriage ecosystems.

This urgent realization inspired him and his wife to gather personal funds and rent a small room directly inside a marginalized pocket opposite the IIT Bombay campus. That single room planted the exact seed that blossomed into the expansive layout of LCCWA in subsequent years.

"His precise legacy ensures that all teachers across LCCWA adopt a highly systematic, analytical, and scientific method while teaching, helping generations of students cultivate an authentic aptitude for science."
