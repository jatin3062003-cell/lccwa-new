About Our Organization

Who We Are

Educate. Heal. Feed. — Uplifting communities and shaping the proactive citizens of tomorrow.

Our Origin Story

LCCWA (Life Care & Child Welfare Association) was founded in 2012 by a group of eminent citizens of Powai, including retired professors of IIT Bombay, to work deliberately towards the development and holistic upliftment of children living in nearby marginalized areas.

LCCWA enables comprehensive all-round growth by providing consistent daily classroom coaching, weekly computer training labs, free freshly cooked nutritious meals every single evening, and regular preventive health check-ups paired with sanitation and hygiene education.

Apart from systematically improving localized academic test performance, our center purposefully lowers school dropout rates and directly imparts vital social communication workflows alongside a positive, humane value system. We believe these durable foundational qualities are what truly empower children to transform into responsible and proactive citizens of the country.

The Directive Mandate

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide consistent academic, emotional, and nutritional support to children from Powai’s marginalized sections. We strive to facilitate holistic development to children who come from economically challenged backgrounds.

We firmly believe there is absolutely no lack of natural talent in children from any background; what they need more than anything, however, is a safe, compassionate, and nurturing ecosystem where they can easily discover and tap into their immense latent potential.

Our Core Values

The ethical cornerstones behind every initiative

Compassion and Care

We treat every individual child with total kindness and profound respect, putting their long-term health, psychological wellbeing, and individual growth directly at the heart of all we do.

Integrity and Discipline

We act with absolute structural fairness, accountability, and organizational responsibility, curating a completely safe, stable, and transparent learning environment.

Active Collaboration

We build deeply rooted alignments alongside neighborhood families, institutional educators, and external community partners to support children and cement lasting generational impact.

Excellence and Learning

We continually strive for premier quality programs and transparent metric outcomes, effectively blending daily education formats with functional real-world workforce competencies.
