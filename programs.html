Our Academic Program

Providing high-impact educational frameworks to bridge learning gaps for underprivileged youth.

Problem Statement

Children living in Mumbai’s low-income areas lack the basic facilities needed for them to learn, grow and become responsible citizens. With almost a quarter of all children in Mumbai living in these conditions, the future of the city will likely be defined by how it takes care of their development.

The post-pandemic landscape has further escalated challenges due to inflation, educational gaps, and loss of key household livelihoods.

Background of Beneficiaries

Children living in the bustees of Powai come from challenging socio-economic situations. Their parents mostly work as construction workers, house-maids, auto-rickshaw drivers, labourers, or street vendors. Many are raised by resilient single mothers. What they share in common is minimal educational resources and an uncertain future.

Comprehensive Education Model

We run throughout the year and conduct daily teaching sessions for 3rd - 12th grade (taking a month-long vacation in May). We teach students across the Maharashtra State Board, covering English, Marathi, Hindi, and Semi-English mediums. Admissions for 11th std start shortly after 10th board results are announced.

Rigorous Assessment Paths

We conduct rigorous selection testing for older students who wish to pursue the Science stream to ensure they can manage the intensive syllabus, alongside assessing Arts and Commerce students for foundational Mathematics mastery.

Our full-time teachers are ably supported by an excellent team of dedicated volunteers mostly hailing from the local Powai community and the IIT Bombay NSS group.

30+ Professional Educators
300+ Enrolled Students
100% SSC & HSC Pass Rate

By the generosity of our donors, we have recently acquired 29 state-of-the-art desktop computers on which children from class 5 to 10 learn computer operations. Classrooms are fully WiFi-enabled with smart LED TVs.

Challenges Beyond Academics

Holistic intervention strategies

Health, Nutrition & Hygiene

Inadequate nutrition and lack of hygiene awareness—especially menstrual health education for young girls—directly limits classroom concentration.

Limited Exposure & Skills

A historical lack of extracurricular activities and basic computer access prevents children from achieving comprehensive potential milestones.

Challenging Environments

Difficult neighborhood surroundings render vulnerable youths susceptible to local negative, counterproductive systemic behaviors.
