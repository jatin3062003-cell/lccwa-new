WELCOME

LCCWA is a Mumbai-based non-profit organization dedicated to uplifting children from underprivileged communities in and around Powai. We work with students who attend overcrowded municipal and low-resourced private schools, where limited individual attention often affects learning outcomes. By providing supplementary academic support along with value-based education, we help bridge these gaps and nurture holistic development. Our mission is to enable children to learn more effectively, build confidence, develop strong values, and acquire the skills needed to create brighter, more independent futures for themselves and their communities.

Every day, together with you, LCCWA works to drive positive change, enriching the lives of its students and empowering women to create a brighter future.

Our work

What We Do

✎

REGULAR TEACHING

We run daily classes for students of pre-primary to class 12, with special focus on Maths and Science.

✿

DAILY NUTRITION

A dedicated team of ladies cook nutritious meals for our students every day.

⚗

SCIENCE LAB

Our science lessons are complemented by hands-on experiments conducted at our science lab for better clarity of concepts.

⌘

COMPUTER LAB

Students from classes 5 to 10 attend daily computer lab sessions to develop their understanding of computers and software programs.

♥

WOMEN EMPOWERMENT

At Shakti, our women's wing, we teach women skills that would make them financially independent.

LCCWA Sports Day 2025

Celebrating Fitness, Fun, and Teamwork. On 21st December, LCCWA celebrated Sports Day with great enthusiasm. Students took part in races, Kho-Kho, Kabaddi, lemon and spoon race, and cricket.

Grade 11 and 12 volunteers helped organize the events. Snacks and juice were served, and all participants received medals and trophies.
