Non-profit in Powai, Mumbai

Every child deserves a bright future.

LCCWA supports children from underprivileged communities in and around Powai with extra classes, daily meals and value-based education, so they can learn well, grow in confidence and build independent futures.

Our core mission

Many of our students attend overcrowded municipal and low-resourced private schools, where individual attention is limited. We bridge that gap with supplementary academic support and value-based education for holistic development.

"Every day, together with you, LCCWA works to drive positive change, enriching the lives of its students and empowering women to create a brighter future."

Recent events

LCCWA Sports Day 2025
21 December 2025

Celebrating fitness, fun and teamwork

On 21 December, LCCWA celebrated Sports Day with great enthusiasm. Supported by Bharat Cares under SMEC Trust and HDFC's Parivartan Small Grants Programme, students took part in races, Kho-Kho, Kabaddi, lemon and spoon race, and cricket.

Grade 11 and 12 volunteers helped organize the events. The cricket match was a highlight, with Grade 9 winning. Snacks and juice were served, and all participants received medals and trophies. We thank our sponsors for making the event successful.
