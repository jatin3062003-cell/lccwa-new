LCCWA Sports Day 2025
21 December 2025

Celebrating fitness, fun and teamwork

On 21 December, LCCWA celebrated Sports Day with great enthusiasm. Students took part in races, Kho-Kho, Kabaddi, lemon and spoon race, and cricket.

Grade 11 and 12 volunteers helped organize the events, with snacks, medals and trophies for all participants.
