Our story

LCCWA is a Mumbai-based non-profit organization dedicated to uplifting children from underprivileged communities in and around Powai. We provide supplementary academic support and value-based education to help children build confidence, develop strong values, and acquire skills for independent futures.

Every day, together with families, educators, and volunteers, we work to create brighter opportunities for students and women in our community.

“Together with you, LCCWA works to drive positive change and empower young people to create a brighter future.”
