About Our Association

Founded by a dedicated ecosystem from IIT Bombay & local Powai citizens.

Our Origin

Founded in 2012 by a proactive collective of local Powai citizens, retired faculty educators, and visionary alumni from IIT Bombay, LCCWA operates explicitly to bridge sharp educational inequalities[cite: 2].

We step in directly where overcrowding in public municipalities or under-resourced private institutions prevents children and local women from securing their true potential[cite: 2, 3].

Our Core Dual Mission

  • Provide comprehensive educational, nutritional, and digital scaffolding to students up to Grade 12[cite: 3, 4, 5].
  • Equip community women with vocational dignity and economic independence to foster stable, self-reliant households[cite: 2, 6].
