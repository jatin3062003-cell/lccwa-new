Early Childhood Wing

Ankur Pre-Primary School

Nurturing tender seedlings to help them sprout into resilient, capable, and vibrant individuals.

The Foundational Stepping Stone

Ankur signifies a tender seedling that, with attentive care and regular nutrition, will blossom into a strong, resilient tree over time. This beautiful philosophy defines our dedicated Pre-Primary School branch at LCCWA.

Early childhood development is where young minds capture fundamental developmental concepts, creating a stable core framework for better reception of downstream higher academic syllabi.

Inaugurated on September 5, 2023, Ankur bridges the early developmental learning gap, serving as an vital prerequisite program directly linked into our established standard Remedial schooling wings.

25+ Active Sprouting Students

Our Core Curriculum Focus

Nurturing growth using proven pedagogical principles

Montessori Principles

Self-directed, structured activity environments that guide kids toward physical development and natural comprehension milestones.

Humane Values

Instilling core cultural values of foundational social responsibility, discipline, collaborative play, and deep emotional empathy.

Essential Life Skills

Cultivating communication accuracy, creative problem-solving capabilities, and independent focus to safely unlock potential.

The Ankur Educator Team

SG

Sutapa Ghosh

Founder of Ankur

Deeply passionate about child psychology and teaching systems, Sutapa brings over 15 years of robust hands-on expertise in early educational design. Inspired by an unwavering goal to serve marginalized cohorts, she founded the Ankur branch on September 5, 2023, integrating core Montessori mechanics with structural civic values.

SM

Sudipta Mukherjee Mandal

Creative Educator

Sudipta is a dynamic, creative educator, specialized artist, and community development advocate. She holds academic degrees in Sociology and Education, supplemented by specialized advanced diplomas in Early Childhood Education. Closely linked with LCCWA since its foundational inception, she designs unique visual spaces inside Ankur that promote balance, creative expression, and student empathy.

We are excited for the steady progress and the transformation of these bright young minds in the years ahead!
